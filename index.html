About TechBridge

Where learning meets real work

TechBridge, by Baselink Services Limited, helps learners close the gap between learning a skill and using it in the real world, through practical, project-based experience.

What we offer

  • Practical programs in Data Analytics and Web Development
  • A 30-day internship built around hands-on project work
  • The opportunity to earn a recommendation letter based on your performance
  • A community of learners to grow with

Who it's for

  • Students and recent graduates who want practical experience
  • Learners ready to build skills through real projects
  • Anyone looking to move from learning to doing

The Internship

A 30-day practical internship

The TechBridge internship is designed to give you real, hands-on experience. Instead of only learning theory, you work on practical, project-based tasks that help you build real skills.

  • Project-based learning: learn by doing, through practical tasks.
  • Hands-on experience: apply your skills in Data Analytics or Web Development.
  • Recommendation letter opportunity: available based on successful participation and performance.
